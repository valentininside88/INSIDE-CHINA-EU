Sea Freight

China → EU · Major ports

FCL (20', 40', 40'HC) and LCL groupage from all major Chinese ports. Weekly LCL consolidation at our Riga hub, onward delivery to any EU country.

Best for: regular shipments, price-sensitive cargo, larger volumes

Rail Freight

China → EU · Faster than sea

China–Europe block trains from all major origin points. Faster than sea, more economical than air. FCL and LCL options.

Best for: time-sensitive cargo where sea is too slow and air too expensive

Air Freight

China → EU · Urgent shipments

Express to any major EU airport. For samples, high-value cargo, and contract deadlines that no other mode can meet.

Best for: small volumes, high-value goods, urgent deadlines

Road Delivery

Any EU address · Door delivery

From Riga hub or any EU port of entry to any address across Europe. FTL and LTL. Coordinated with customs — one handoff, no gaps.

Best for: last-mile after sea/rail/air, or EU redistribution from our Riga hub

Operations

Two Bases.
One Corridor.

We operate from Riga (HQ) and maintain teams in Hangzhou, Sanya (Hainan Free Trade Port) and Hong Kong — with an agent network across every major manufacturing province in China. The China-side teams handle factory pickups, FCR, and pre-shipment checks. The Riga team manages EU customs, consolidation, and onward delivery across all of Europe.

Hangzhou, Sanya & Hong Kong — problems caught before departure

Factory pickup coordination, FCR issuance, document checks — handled in China before cargo loads. Problems caught at origin cost nothing.

Riga hub — EU deconsolidation & distribution base

LCL cargo arrives at our Riga hub in EU customs zone. Storage, repacking, labelling, and onward dispatch to any EU country.

In-house customs LV — partners across all EU entry points

We clear in Latvia directly. Partners at major EU ports — we clear at the most practical entry point for each shipment.
